Three visual directions and the next machines

The references are Dribbble ("car HUD", "simulation UI", "engineering dashboard"), Bartosz Ciechanowski's engine article, and Teenage Engineering product pages. Each direction below shows the look, the motion ideas and the cost.

Recommended

A · Toy cutaway + hardware deck

Each part gets one flat, saturated colour with a black outline, like the Ciechanowski engine. The model sits on warm paper. The controls look like a real device in the Teenage Engineering style: round knobs with detents, coloured keys, and charts on small black "LCD" screens.

  • Fluids as coloured particles: blue air, yellow mixture, orange flame, grey exhaust.
  • Exploded-view toggle: parts fly apart along their axes with an eased motion.
  • Chapter tour: tap "Compressor" and the camera moves there, then the part is outlined.
  • Charts draw like an oscilloscope trace, with a short fading tail behind the dot.

Looks hand-made, not AI-generated · cost: medium (toon shading, outline pass, new control skin)

B · Night-mode instrument HUD

The Dribbble car-HUD look: graphite background, glowing wireframes, arc gauges, and scrolling telemetry strips. Hot parts glow with a bloom effect.

  • Numbers count up when they change. Gauges sweep with spring physics.
  • Telemetry strips scroll in real time, like an oscilloscope.
  • Knock or a cabinet hit makes a red pulse and a short screen shake.

Striking on a portfolio · risk: this is the most common Dribbble style, so it can still look generated · cost: medium

C · Living engineering drawing

The machine appears as a technical drawing that comes alive. It has ink lines, section hatching, dimension arrows and a title block. Charts are "figures" with figure numbers.

  • The drawing "inks itself" (line draw-on) when a lab opens.
  • Section cuts slide across the part to reveal the inside.
  • Dimensions update live as you drag (stroke, gap, compression height).

Very engineering, very calm · risk: less "wow" in the first 5 s on a phone · cost: medium-high (line rendering)

References seen: ciechanow.ski / Internal Combustion Engine (part colours, inline sliders, cutaways, pause-all toggle) · teenage.engineering OP-1 (hardware controls, LCD line art) · Dribbble: car HUD dashboard and simulation UI (glow, arc gauges, telemetry) · Awwwards 3D (scroll and transition motion).
